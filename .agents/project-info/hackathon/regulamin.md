# Task rules (regulamin): prize, jury, IP
The legal frame of the PRAWDOWID task. (source: sources/2026-11-21-msz-regulamin-zadania.pdf)

- **Event:** HackNation 2026, organized by Ministerstwo Cyfryzacji (Warsaw). The task "PRAWDOWID" is set by MSZ, which is the "Przyrzekający" (prize promisor, art. 919 §1 KC), represented by the Director of the MSZ Biuro Transformacji Cyfrowej. The event-wide rules are at www.hacknation.pl. If they conflict, **this task regulamin wins** (§5.2).
- **Eligibility:** registered HackNation 2026 participants only.
- **Jury (§2):** appointed by MSZ, with at least 3 members who are experts in the subject. The lineup is published no later than the event start day. The criteria were published with the full task at 11:00 on day 1 (see [judging-and-metrics.md](judging-and-metrics.md)). **Decisions are final, with no appeal.**
- **Prize (§3):** **one main prize: 30,000 PLN gross** (tax withheld by MSZ). It is split equally among team members unless all of them jointly declare otherwise, and paid within 7 days of signing the copyright transfer. It can't be exchanged or transferred.
- **IP (§4):**
  - Each participant declares authorship of their contribution, and that the solution has no legal defects and doesn't infringe third-party rights.
  - **Winners must sign a transfer of economic copyright to MSZ** (written form, art. 53 of the Copyright Act). All co-authors must sign within 7 days of receiving the contract, or the prize can go to the next team.
  - The fields of exploitation cover software (art. 74 ust. 4) and other works (art. 50), including the source code.
- **Implication:** use only permissively licensed dependencies and data we may redistribute, and record those licenses (they are also a deliverable).
