# Target audience
Who uses PRAWDOWID and what each group needs. (source: sources/2026-11-21-msz-pelna-tresc-zadania.pdf, sec. 1, 3, 9)

## Primary: the citizen
- Any Polish resident who meets a rumour, post, link or screenshot and wants to know if it's true. **This includes people with low digital skills**, who shouldn't need to learn verification methods or install anything.
- Needs: a fast answer (target ≤ 30 s), **plain Polish**, free of charge, in a channel they already use, a clear verdict *or* an honest "we don't know", sources they can open themselves, and readable contrast on a phone screen.
- The system should **encourage them to look at the evidence**, not just trust a label (sec. 9).

## Secondary: moderator or analyst (system admin)
- An MSZ moderator or analyst, designated by the admin.
- Needs: alerts on **high-risk** reports and on **repeated similar questions with no answer**, one deduplicated case per cluster, an audit log of checks, data export (CSV/JSON), and later OpenCTI and DISARM integration.
- For the pilot: **decision-support mode**, where a human approves answers that get published.

## Stakeholder: MSZ (the client) and the jury
- MSZ wants something that can grow into a pilot: auditable, low-risk, EU-friendly hosting, transparent about AI, and aligned with IFCN, EFCSN and EDMO standards. Details: [pitch/jury-insights.md](../pitch/jury-insights.md).
